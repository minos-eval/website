## Scope

Single-page MINOS Evals showcase, Persuade mode, Turkish at `/`, English at `/en`. Full decisions: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-minos-evals-site-design.md`.

Audience: people with an internal AI assistant, technically literate and skeptical. Success: they think "we should evaluate ours like this". No primary action, contact sits at the bottom.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A real 19th-century mural of Minos is the whole identity. The page refuses the category's product-screenshot hero and its feature grid. The judge looks at the sentence, and that sentence says the last word is yours.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black ground #140c07 sampled from the painting's shadows, cream text #f3e9dd, one coral accent #ff5f6d used only where a human or needs_review matters. Commissioner, one family, flared at FLAR 100 for titles. Hairlines, no cards, no icons. The real painting and its red drapery are the only images.

STORY: The visitor learns why ordinary tests fail on LLM apps, how the flow works, that a rule makes the verdict, why the name is Minos, how much say the judge has, how access grows, and that answers stay with their own models.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Painting anchored left, about three quarters of the viewport wide, head and shoulders, dissolving to the ground to the right and below. Title and lead sit to its right at gaze height. Fixed tabs run along the top right. No button.

FORM: Pinned by the user, chosen from three comps as "A · Karanlık". Signature interaction: on scroll the painting shrinks into the fixed menu bar and leaves the strip with the eye as the bar's background. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
